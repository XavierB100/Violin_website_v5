About Dorothé

Experience. Heart. Excellence.

Classically trained violinist with over 40 years of performance experience across Europe and North America.

  • Wedding ceremonies & receptions
  • Funeral services and memorials
  • Corporate events, worship services and seasonal celebrations
  • Studio recording sessions, remote or in person

My performances blend technical precision with emotional depth to perfectly complement your occasion, whether live or in the studio. With extensive improvisation experience across many music genres, I can adapt each performance to your needs.

My Musical Journey

A lifelong devotion to music

1970s

Early Beginnings

Began playing at age 7 in Rotterdam. Joined church worship teams and the Rotterdam Youth Symphony Orchestra by age 12.

1980s-1990s

International Performances

Played with bands across the Netherlands, Belgium, France and America, recording albums and ministering through music. Performed with the Christian Radio and TV Orchestra.

2000s-Present

Current Work

Member of Brent Symphony Orchestra, session violinist and active worship musician at HTB London. Performed at over 200 events.
